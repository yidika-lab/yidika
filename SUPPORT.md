# Getting help

- **How do I...?** Ask in [Discussions](https://github.com/yidika-lab/yidika/discussions), category Q&A.
- **Something is broken.** Open a [bug report](https://github.com/yidika-lab/yidika/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) with a minimal `.yk` program.
- **Security problem.** Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); do not open a public issue.
- **Want to contribute?** Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and pick an issue labelled [good first issue](https://github.com/yidika-lab/yidika/labels/good%20first%20issue).
